# 风格池

一批六张，每张一种风格，六张互不相同。从下表挑选，也可以按检索结果换成同等水准的风格。挑选时让媒介、主色和构图拉开距离，主色调至少三组，都要明亮、积极，同时克制，见 [design.md](design.md#品牌调性)。

| 风格 | prompt 里要写的画面语言 | 文字位置 |
|---|---|---|
| 品牌主视觉 | one hero object centered on a clean bright colour or warm paper background, product-photography lighting, real material texture, generous clean space above | 上方居中 |
| 高速运动摄影 | high-speed flash, one decisive moment frozen mid-air, a single athlete seen from behind or in part, sweat and net in crisp detail, bright arena softly out of focus, low angle | 底部左对齐，或一侧竖排 |
| 纪实体育摄影 | 35mm film, one real moment courtside, bright arena, crowd only as soft background, natural grain | 印在画面里的横幅、记分牌或场边屏幕上 |
| 静物摄影 | high-key studio still life, one object on a bright clean backdrop, hard sunlight shadows, real material texture | 主体下方居中，字小 |
| 瑞士字体海报 | Swiss grid, one enormous condensed grotesk word, two bright flat inks on white, strict margins | 主文案就是网格里的大字，落款对齐网格 |
| 波普插画 | pop art print, Ben-Day dots, flat bright primaries, one bold graphic object, no cartoon faces | 字就是画面的一部分，或斜排大字 |
| Risograph 双色印刷 | two-colour risograph in fluorescent pink, orange or yellow ink, uncoated paper, halftone dots, slight misregistration | 用第二色印成标题 |
| 复古票根与场刊 | letterpress in bright spot colours on cream card stock, ink spread, perforation, creases, overhead photo | 票面或场刊封面的标题位 |
| 杂志封面 | editorial cover layout, masthead-style headline, one subject, bright colour background | 刊头下方的封面大标题 |
| 版画 | woodcut or linocut, carved marks, two or three bright spot colours, confident cutting strokes | 和画面一起刻出来的题字 |
| 纸艺浮雕 | layered paper relief in bright coloured card, strong side light, playful depth | 纸面上方压印 |
| 微缩模型 | believable miniature diorama, one clear scene, bright daylight colours, shallow depth of field | 印在模型里的横幅或记分牌上 |
| 极简几何 | constructivist shapes, bright flat colour, one shape carries the metaphor, strong diagonal composition | 与主形状对齐 |
| 东方留白 | ink wash on xuan paper with vivid vermilion or gold accents, fast flying-white brushstrokes that carry motion；只用于中国节日和节气 | 一侧竖排，Logoman 落在印章位 |

## 文案

每张都有主文案和节日落款，写法与版式见 [copy.md](copy.md)。风格决定文字放在哪里、用什么字体气质。六张的文字位置至少三种，上方居中最多两张。字体海报、波普插画、杂志封面这类版式作品，主文案可以就是画面的主角。
