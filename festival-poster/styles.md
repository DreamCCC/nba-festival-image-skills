# 风格池

一批六张，每张一种风格，六张互不相同。从下表挑选，也可以按检索结果换成同等水准的风格。挑选时让媒介、主色和构图拉开距离，主色调至少三组，都要明亮、积极，见 [design.md](design.md#品牌调性)。

| 风格 | prompt 里要写的画面语言 | 文案位置 |
|---|---|---|
| 高速运动摄影 | high-speed flash, peak action frozen mid-air, sweat and net in crisp detail, bright arena lights, low angle | 画面上方一行粗体大字 |
| 纪实体育摄影 | 35mm film, packed bright arena, crowd on its feet, motion blur, natural grain | 底部一行醒目的粗体字，像报纸头条 |
| 静物摄影 | high-key studio still life, bright saturated backdrop, hard sunlight shadows, real material texture | 底部一行小字，像展览图录的图注 |
| 瑞士字体海报 | Swiss grid, one enormous condensed grotesk word, two bright flat inks on white, strict margins | 文案就是主标题，占据网格 |
| 波普插画 | pop art illustration, bold outlines, Ben-Day dots, flat saturated primaries, dynamic diagonal composition | 对话框或斜排的大字 |
| Risograph 双色印刷 | two-colour risograph in fluorescent pink, orange or yellow ink, uncoated paper, halftone dots, slight misregistration | 用第二色印成标题 |
| 复古票根与场刊 | letterpress in bright spot colours on cream card stock, ink spread, perforation, creases, overhead photo | 印在票面或场刊封面的标题位 |
| 杂志封面 | editorial cover layout, masthead-style headline, one subject in motion, bright colour background | 封面主标题 |
| 版画 | woodcut or linocut, carved marks, two or three bright spot colours, energetic cutting strokes | 和画面一起刻出来的题字 |
| 纸艺浮雕 | layered paper relief in bright coloured card, strong side light, playful depth | 压印在纸面下方 |
| 微缩模型 | believable miniature diorama of a lively packed arena, bright daylight colours, shallow depth of field | 模型里的一块招牌或横幅 |
| 极简几何 | constructivist shapes, bright flat colour, one shape carries the metaphor, strong diagonal motion | 与主形状对齐的一行字 |
| 东方留白 | ink wash on xuan paper with vivid vermilion or gold accents, fast flying-white brushstrokes that carry motion；只用于中国节日和节气 | 竖排题字，旁边落 Logoman 印章 |

## 文案

每张都有一句文案，写法见 [copy.md](copy.md)。风格只决定文案出现在哪里、用什么字体气质：

- 摄影类作品的文案是一行醒目但不抢主体的字。
- 字体海报、波普插画、杂志封面、票根这类版式作品，文案可以是画面的主角。
